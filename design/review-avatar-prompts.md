## Общая часть

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square portrait avatar for a clearly labeled demonstration testimonial card on a Russian architectural home-building company website. Create a realistic photograph of ONE FICTIONAL adult, not a celebrity or real customer. Head and upper shoulders, face centered, entire hair/head visible with comfortable margin, crop safe for a small circular avatar. Natural relaxed expression with a subtle friendly smile, direct gaze, realistic skin with small imperfections, soft natural window light, gentle depth of field. Understated everyday clothing and muted neutral surroundings. A believable personal profile photograph, not a glamorous fashion portrait or stock corporate headshot. No text, logo, watermark, borders, collage or additional people. Square 1024x1024.
